# Chinese Science Copywriting

## Hard Rules
- Use Chinese social-readable titles, not paper titles.
- Prefer verbs and consequences.
- Keep module headings short: 6-14 Chinese characters when possible.
- Put uncertainty and caveats on the image when they prevent misinterpretation.
- Do not use absolute claims such as `证明`, `必然`, `完全`, or `唯一机制` unless the source directly supports them.
- Use `提示`, `注意`, `局限`, or `别这样读` for caution modules.

## Bad To Better
- `PCA 在代谢组学中的应用` -> `PCA 图到底在看什么？`
- `Western blot 结果解释` -> `WB 条带不是越黑越好`
- `Molecular docking score interpretation` -> `对接分数别这么解读`
