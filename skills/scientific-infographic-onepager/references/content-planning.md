# Content Planning

## Compression Ladder
1. Core scientific claim in one sentence.
2. Reader promise in one sentence.
3. Evidence shape: schematic, real data, paper result, workflow, comparison, or correction.
4. Module map: 4-7 modules for one long image.
5. Copy compression: titles, short fragments, labels, caveats.

## Content Router
- `method-primer`: method or analysis concept.
- `mechanism-map`: biological mechanism, pathway, causal chain, drug action.
- `myth-vs-fact`: common misunderstanding and corrected interpretation.
- `paper-digest`: one paper's finding, design, evidence, caveat, and takeaway.
- `workflow-map`: experimental or computational pipeline.
- `comparison-matrix`: compare 2-4 methods, tools, choices, or interpretations.

## Chinese Hook Patterns
- `PCA 图到底在看什么？`
- `WB 条带不是越黑越好`
- `分子对接分数别这么解读`
- `UMAP 聚类不是细胞类型证明`
- `这篇论文真正回答了什么？`
